Frequently Asked Questions

What is CrimznBot?

CrimznBot is an AI assistant built to answer crypto questions with live token prices, market insights, and strategic analysis.

How does the paywall work?

You get 3 free questions total. After that, you can unlock full, unlimited access with a payment of 0.025 SOL using Solana Pay (preferred).

Do I need a wallet to use CryptoConsult?

Yes. Phantom wallet is recommended. You'll be asked to connect your wallet to track usage, unlock features, and save your profile.

Is this financial advice?

No. CrimznBot provides consultation and insights only. Nothing shared is financial advice. Always DYOR.

What payments are accepted?

You can pay via Solana Pay (preferred), Coinbase Commerce (USDC), or PayPal (@crimzn).

What is $CONSOL?

$CONSOL is CryptoConsult’s planned on-chain utility token. It may be used for unlocking CrimznBot, gaining access to premium tools, sponsoring development, or participating in governance. Future use cases are still being explored.

Will CryptoKids CryptoQuiz be integrated?

Yes. CryptoKids CryptoQuiz is planned as an interactive educational module, allowing users to connect their wallet, play, and potentially earn rewards or tokens in the future.

What other tools are included?

CryptoConsult includes CrimznBot (AI Q&A with live token prices), PulseIt (sentiment analyzer), and Market Radar (market-top detection dashboards). New Software-as-a-Service tools are in development.
