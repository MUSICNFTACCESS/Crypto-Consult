Frequently Asked Questions

🤖 What is CrimznBot?

CrimznBot is an AI assistant built to answer crypto questions with live token prices, market insights, and strategy breakdowns. It reflects Crimzn’s personal tone—sharp, based, and brutally helpful.

🔓 How does the paywall work?

You get 3 free questions. After that, you'll need to unlock access with a payment of 0.025 SOL using Solana Pay. The wallet is verified using on-chain logic via Helius and Firebase.

👛 Do I need a wallet to use CryptoConsult?

Yes. Phantom wallet is recommended. You'll be asked to connect your wallet to track usage, unlock features, and optionally save your profile (name & email).

🧠 Is this financial advice?

No. CrimznBot provides consultation and insights only. Nothing shared is financial advice. Always DYOR.

💳 What payments are accepted?

You can pay via Solana Pay (preferred), Coinbase Commerce (USDC), or PayPal (@crimzn).
