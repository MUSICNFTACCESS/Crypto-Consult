📜 Introduction

CryptoConsult is a blockchain-native consultation platform built for investors, traders, and learners seeking professional, AI-enhanced guidance to bridge education, empowerment, and execution in the crypto space through intuitive tools, real-time insights, and direct access to decentralized rails.

🛠️ Services Offered

  • Technical & fundamental analysis
  • Altcoin rotation strategies
  • Wallet setup and security
  • On-chain analytics & portfolio planning
  • Real-time & off-ramping crypto
  • AI-powered bot support (CrimznBot)

🔐 Token-Gated Access

Users receive 3 free AI questions per session. To unlock full access, payments can be made via Coinbase Commerce, PayPal, or Solana Pay. Wallet authentication is required.

🧩 Integrated Tools

  • 📈 Market Radar: Market-top detection dashboard
  • 📊 PulseIt: Sentiment analyzer with color-coded output
  • 💸 Live Prices: BTC, ETH, and SOL updated in real-time

  • ETF inflow monitoring & dashboards
  • On-chain token rewards for users
  • DAO voting and governance tools
  • Telegram & Discord w/ full access & wallet login
