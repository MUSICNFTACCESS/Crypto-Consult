CryptoConsult by Crimzn

🔍 Introduction

CryptoConsult is a blockchain-native consultation platform built for investors, traders, and learners seeking professional, AI-enhanced guidance through each crypto market cycle.

🧠 Mission

To bridge education, empowerment, and execution in the crypto space through intuitive tools, real-time insights, and direct access to decentralized finance solutions.

📦 Services Offered

  • Technical & fundamental analysis
  • Altcoin rotation strategies
  • Wallet setup and security
  • Capital allocation & portfolio planning
  • On-ramping & off-ramping crypto
  • AI-powered bot support (CrimznBot)

🔐 Token-Gated Access

Users receive 3 free AI questions per session. To unlock full access, payments can be made via Coinbase Commerce, PayPal, or Solana Pay. Wallet connection is available for on-chain authentication.

📡 Integrated Tools

  • 📊 Radar: Market-top detection dashboard
  • 📉 PulseIt: Sentiment analyzer with color-coded output
  • 📈 Live Prices: BTC, ETH, and SOL updated in real-time

🚀 Roadmap

  • ✅ Launch of CrimznBot & paywall system
  • ✅ Coinbase & Solana Pay integrations
  • 📊 ETF flow monitoring & dashboard
  • 🎁 On-chain token rewards for users
  • 🔓 DAO voting and governance tools
  • 📱 Telegram Bot with AI access & wallet login
