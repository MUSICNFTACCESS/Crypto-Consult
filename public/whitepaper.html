Introduction

CryptoConsult is a blockchain-native consultation platform for investors, traders, and learners. We blend market intelligence, AI automation, and Web3 tooling into a fast, secure Solana stack.

Services Offered

  • Technical & fundamental analysis
  • Altcoin rotation strategies
  • Wallet setup & security
  • On-chain analytics & portfolio planning
  • Real-time & soft-prompted GPT responses
  • AI bot support (CrimznBot)
  • Software as a Service (SaaS): custom Web3 dashboards, alerts, and integrations

Access & Paywall

Each user gets 3 free questions with CrimznBot. After that, unlock full access via Solana Pay (0.025 SOL). Free-question counts are stored client-side and enforced server-side.
